After 13 fantastic years, our conductor Leif Karlsson has decided to hand over the baton in spring 2027. We are therefore looking for a new conductor and artistic director.

Uppsala Blåsarsymfoniker was founded in 2000 to be an ambitious amateur orchestra focused on modern original music for wind orchestra, and we also play much of the classic wind band repertoire. We rehearse every Monday in Uppsala and give around two to three concerts per term. With several wins at the Swedish Wind Band Championships and strong results at the European Championship for Wind Orchestras, we are proud to be one of Sweden's leading amateur wind orchestras.

We would like our new conductor to have experience of wind music, to be able to lead a rehearsal once a week, and to be able to conduct the orchestra for at least two years. We look forward to taking the orchestra forward together – and to keep combining high ambitions with the joy of playing!

Interested, or do you know someone who would be a good fit? [Fill in the form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeByXqncQp5iB8Pltxyx3MKFUOA1rHVEVdKzXIHJOCnAJ1KXg/viewform) (in Swedish). For questions, email [info@uppsalablasarsymfoniker.se](mailto:info@uppsalablasarsymfoniker.se).
