January is long, dark, and expensive – and far too dull not to celebrate once it is finally over! On 30 January, Jacke Sjödin hosts Januarikalaset, this year at UKK, with Babben Larsson, Bröderna Rongedal, and a large musical ensemble. Expect an evening of humour, music, and a well-earned end to January – with an extra matinee at 15.00.

Januarikalaset has become an annual tradition in Uppsala, and in 2027 it takes over the Main Hall at UKK. Wordsmith Jacke Sjödin is behind the show and also hosts it. With him are Babben Larsson, as natural a singer as she is a stand-up comedian, and Bröderna Rongedal, the singing twins who move easily between musical genres.

Uppsala Blåsarsymfoniker, conducted by Leif Karlsson, gives the music its full power. Uppsala's own Trio X – Lennart Simonsson, Hans Backenroth, and Jocke Ekberg – is also on stage.

## Performers

- **Orchestra:** Uppsala Blåsarsymfoniker and Trio X
- **Artists:** Jacke Sjödin, Babben Larsson, and Bröderna Rongedal
- **Conductor:** Leif Karlsson

With support from the Municipality of Uppsala.
