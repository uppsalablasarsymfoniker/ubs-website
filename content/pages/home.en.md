Uppsala Blåsarsymfoniker is one of Sweden's leading amateur wind orchestras, and has been part of the rich Swedish wind music tradition since it was founded in 2000. We put great emphasis on original works with a modern sound, but are just as much at home in the classic wind band repertoire – and in collaborations with artists from jazz, soul, and pop.

With a deeply committed membership, we combine the joy of playing with high ambitions.
