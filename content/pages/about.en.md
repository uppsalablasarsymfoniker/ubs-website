Uppsala Blåsarsymfoniker was founded in 2000 by a group of young wind music enthusiasts who wanted to perform original music for wind orchestra. Since then it has grown into one of Sweden's leading amateur wind orchestras, known for its high ambitions and joy of playing. With a strong commitment to original works and modern wind music, we combine the traditional with the new.

We rehearse every Monday in Uppsala and give two to three concerts per term. Our members come from Uppsala, and also from Västerås and Stockholm.

## Collaborations

Over the years we have worked with distinguished musicians and composers, among them Nils Landgren, Christian Lindberg, Johan de Meij, and Jan Van der Roost, and with artists such as Helen Sjöholm, Kristin Amparo, Samuel Ljungblahd, and Trio X.

## Competitions

Competing is part of how the orchestra develops, in Sweden and abroad. We have won the Swedish Wind Band Championships several times, most recently in autumn 2025, took gold at the World Music Contest in Kerkrade in 2017, and gold at the European Championship for Wind Orchestras in 2023 and 2024. In November 2026 we represented Sweden at the European Championship in Stuttgart.

## Conductor

Leif Karlsson has conducted the orchestra since 2013. He was one of the founders of Kroumata Percussion Ensemble, works as music inspector for the Swedish Armed Forces Music, and is a member of the Royal Swedish Academy of Music. After 13 years, Leif hands over the baton in spring 2027, and we are now looking for a new conductor and artistic director.
