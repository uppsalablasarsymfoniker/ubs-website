Uppsala Blåsarsymfoniker is happy to take part in concerts, festivals, and events. We are around 50 musicians and play everything from original works for wind orchestra and classical repertoire to soul, jazz, and pop in new arrangements.

Among other things we have appeared in Januarikalaset, the spring concerts of the choir Allmänna Sången, and concerts at Uppsala Konsert & Kongress, and have worked with choirs, soloists, and other orchestras.

Get in touch and tell us about your event, and we will get back to you.
