Would you like to play with Uppsala Blåsarsymfoniker? We welcome musicians who want to combine high ambitions with the joy of playing.

## How we rehearse

We rehearse every Monday evening in Uppsala and give two to three concerts per term, often together with other artists. From time to time we also compete, in Sweden and abroad. Many of our members commute from Västerås and Stockholm.

## Get in touch

Send us an email telling us about yourself, the instrument you play, and your musical background, and we will get back to you.
